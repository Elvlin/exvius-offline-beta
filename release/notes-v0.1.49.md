**Exvius Offline v0.1.49 (beta)**

- Third-rank Esper trials now load the dungeon map
- Weapon Enhancement now saves the weapon you selected when you tap Fight, so clearing floor can offer three abilities instead of sending you back to Home without a choice.
- Load Party now updates the Weapon Enhancement party, including its units, equipment, and Espers.
- Steel Castle Melfikya floors 2 and 5 now use the correct wave count.
- Material Shop now opens on its own screen with one section, NV Materials. It has 16 items at 100 Lapis each.
- Fixed a malformed mission-data entry that prevented mission 6019003 from loading its map and event script.
- Gil can reach 10,000,000,000.
- Lapis can reach 1,000,000,000.
