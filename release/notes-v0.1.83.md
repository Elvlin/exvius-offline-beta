**Exvius Offline v0.1.83**

**Fixed and updated in this beta**

- Added raids in **Season 2**. **Dark Symphony**, **Conqueror of Izander**, **The Son of Medici**, and **Deus Ex: Mankind Divided**.
- Restored **A Frosty Offensive** in **Season 1** and **The Unsent Fiend** in **Season 4**.
- Added explorations **Trials of Love** and **Into the Abyss** are in Season 1. **Festival of Love** and **Sun, Sand, and Sea** are in Season 2.
- Added **Eternal Winter**, **Winter Mayhem**, **Revelations of Darkness**, **An Eerie Invitation**, and many other event 39 in total.
- Added 40 recipe books to the **Xenostone Shard Exchange**.
- Added the option to pick a companion from any of your ten saved parties. They bring their gear, materia, and Vision Card.
- Moved **5★ Select Summon** to **Summon → Permanent → Standard Summon**.
- Check your mailbox for the free **4th Anniversary Livestream** rewards.
- Fixed Chamber of Awakening
- Corrected boss HP in 19 LGD raids, and many other bugs on raid related content
- Fixed Limit Burst effects for affected forms of **Grahf**, **Master Machinist Abigail**, **Curse of Immunity Melissa**, **Blade of Vengeance Ibara**, and **Ruin Explorer Nora**.

- The old companion has been replaced with 18 new companions as a thank-you to @Patreon Supporter, @Donor and @Moderator who’ve helped keep FFBE Offline going.

I really appreciate everything you’ve done, and to the whole community for being involved and supporting the project so far.

More companions may be added in future updates.
