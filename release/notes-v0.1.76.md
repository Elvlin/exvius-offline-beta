**Exvius Offline v0.1.76 Beta**

New versions come to the beta first. Once players have tested a build and it’s stable, we’ll release it in the main app.

**[Download the beta](https://github.com/Elvlin/exvius-offline-beta/)**

The beta installs **next to** your main app. It has its own storage and downloads its own copy of the game data. Your main app and save stay in place.

**Play your main save in the beta**

1. In the **main app**, go to **Save management → Export save**. Your backup goes to *Download/Exvius Saves*.
2. In the **beta app**, go to **Save management → Import save** and pick that file.

There’s also a fresh [**test save**](https://github.com/Elvlin/exvius-offline-beta/blob/main/save/exvius-beta-test-save.zip) with maxed Lapis, Gil, summon tickets, and raid coins.

**Fixed and updated in this beta**

- Fixed the connection error at the end of story and Vortex battles after accepting **In Search of Kupo Nuts** in the Farplane.
- Fixed the crash when opening **Arena Medal Exchange**. The original medal grid opens, including on saves that have yet to unlock a tile.
- Fixed both **Monster in a Box** encounters in **Daring Rescue at the Crumbling House** so the original Nightwalker battle loads.
- Corrected the **EX Awakening** costs for Master Machinist Abigail, Curse of Immunity Melissa, Blade of Vengeance Ibara, Dark Knight Duane, and Ruin Explorer Nora. Each now uses their own fragments at all three EX ranks. Also corrected duplicate conversion and awakening fragment costs for Rocbouquet and other affected units.
- Fixed the crash when selecting **Vision Cards** in Sell mode. The Sell button sits above the card count and each selection number stays on its card.
- Restored missing bonus payouts on **79 summon offers**. Banners with a matching exchange now have an **Exchange Shop** button below Drop Rates. The shortcut opens the coin or ticket exchange for that banner.
- Moved **116 summon-linked exchanges** out of the standalone Fragment Shops carousel. The remaining fragment shops are still available there.
- Fixed padding on **61 summon banners** and restored four **Exchanges Available** banners covering the two Dark Matter exchanges, Red Star Quartz, and the Super Trust Moogle Ticket Exchange.
- Restored the original **VIP Coin**, **EX Coin**, and **Daily Coin** prices. The **Material Shop** uses Lapis and has separate pages for NV Materials, Tickets, Fragments, and Boosts.

**Updating an existing beta install**

Install the new beta APK and open the app to apply **data revision 25**. Your existing beta save carries over.

Please report bugs with the in-game **Bug Report** button.

*For anyone using the source in their own project, the source will be updated when this release moves to the main repo.*
