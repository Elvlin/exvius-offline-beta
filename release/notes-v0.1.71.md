**Exvius Offline v0.1.71 Beta**

New versions come to the beta first. Once players have tested a build and it’s stable, we’ll release it in the main app.

**[Download the beta](https://github.com/Elvlin/exvius-offline-beta/)**

The beta installs **next to** your main app. It has its own storage and downloads its own copy of the game data. Your main app and save stay in place.

**Play your main save in the beta**

1. In the **main app**, go to **Save management → Export save**. Your backup goes to *Download/Exvius Saves*.
2. In the **beta app**, go to **Save management → Import save** and pick that file.

There’s also a fresh [**test save**](https://github.com/Elvlin/exvius-offline-beta/blob/main/save/exvius-beta-test-save.zip) with maxed Lapis, Gil, summon tickets, and raid coins.

**Fixed and updated in this beta**

- Fixed crashes when opening awakening materials and closing the awakening result.
- Restored missing unit forms and awakening paths. This includes Rubicante and Barbariccia’s NV forms, EX Awakening for affected units, and EX Bonus options for units that support them.
- Corrected the NV+ sprites for Dark Knight Duane, Master Machinist Abigail, Curse of Immunity Melissa, and Ruin Explorer Nora.
- Added **A2 (jp)** to the NieR summon pools alongside A2. Her Trust Master Reward appears as **Beastlord (jp)**.
- Fixed kill counts for town quests like **Bleeder of the Pack**.
- Six Panel Quests that previously required friends can now be completed offline by spending in-game Lapis.
- Fixed party equipment so changing a weapon leaves its equipment in other parties intact.
- Fixed **Steel Castle Melfikya** so it enhances the exact weapon copy you select.
- Restored missing summon banners and organized the tabs. Raid summons have their own tab with season filters, collaboration banners stay under Collab, and step-ups appear under Limited Time.
- Restored the **King Mog Raid Summon**.
- Organized seven event groups, including Chronicle Battle, Vault of Hidden Treasures, and Chamber of Challenges. Dark Visions banners are numbered from the oldest edition to the newest.
- Restored **Doga’s Grotto - Exploration** and **Lands of Plenty** to the event list with their own banners. Great Nian Beast now appears with the correct model and English name, and the Dark Castle route features Rabite and Belladonna.

Please report bugs with the in-game **Bug Report** button.

*For anyone using the source in their own project, the source will be updated when this release moves to the main repo.*
