**Exvius Offline v0.1.74 Beta**

New versions come to the beta first. Once players have tested a build and it’s stable, we’ll release it in the main app.

**[Download the beta](https://github.com/Elvlin/exvius-offline-beta/)**

The beta installs **next to** your main app. It has its own storage and downloads its own copy of the game data. Your main app and save stay in place.

**Play your main save in the beta**

1. In the **main app**, go to **Save management → Export save**. Your backup goes to *Download/Exvius Saves*.
2. In the **beta app**, go to **Save management → Import save** and pick that file.

There’s also a fresh [**test save**](https://github.com/Elvlin/exvius-offline-beta/blob/main/save/exvius-beta-test-save.zip) with maxed Lapis, Gil, summon tickets, and raid coins.

**Fixed and updated in this beta**

- Fixed the **Clash of Wills** Collect button and a startup crash affecting saves with certain EX Coin rewards.
- Fixed **Panel Quest** rewards for completing horizontal, vertical, and diagonal lines. Earned line rewards are available when you open the panel on an existing save, and each reward can be claimed once.
- Summon cards that share Step-Up artwork now show their actual offer type, including Daily Half-Price, Featured Summon, 10+1 Summon, and Treasure Replica. Step-Up pulls advance their own steps, full-price pulls preserve your daily discount, and exhausted offers show zero remaining and preserve your Lapis.
- Daily summon offers reset at **local midnight**, and weekly offers reset at **local midnight on Monday**. Closing and reopening the game keeps your usage for the current period.
- Moved 29 crossover offers to **Collab**, including Dragon Quest, Emblem of Roto, and Collection of Mana. **Counterattack of the Exostars** now appears under **Raid Summon**.
- Updated 87 raid encounters, including the boss stats for **Sovereign Rock Castle**. Raid milestones are marked as awarded when their rewards reach the message box.
- Restored two **Farplane** quest scenes, including **Find the Moogle Apprentices**.
- Fixed stale weapons and gear when switching between regular parties. Saved party presets still load their recorded gear.
- Restored duplicate-to-fragment conversion for affected units, including **High Summoner Braska**, **Magitek Warrior Terra**, **Xezat**, and **Zile**.
- Corrected **Kaito’s** prism awakening so regular Kaito displays as **7★**.
- Fixed a crash when claiming the final **Expedition** tutorial reward and made the enhancement board available as soon as you unlock a new Esper.

Please report bugs with the in-game **Bug Report** button.

*For anyone using the source in their own project, the source will be updated when this release moves to the main repo.*
