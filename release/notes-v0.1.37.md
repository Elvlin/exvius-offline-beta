**Exvius Offline v0.1.37 (beta)**

This is a beta build. It installs beside the main app as a separate app ("com.exvius.vision.elvlin") with its own storage, so your main save is not touched. To play your own progress here, export it from the main app (Save management > Export save) and import it in the beta.

**Fixes**
- The Lapis in a mission's first-clear reward is paid on every clear, so cleared stages can be replayed to farm it.
- Summons with a limit now stop at that limit: the 8-per-week NV and seasonal video summons, and the lap limit of step-up summons.
- The free step of the NV Premium step-up summons is free again.
- Random encounters on exploration maps no longer end like a boss fight.
- Kill quests now count the enemies you defeat.
- Step-up summon banners show their artwork after the first step.
- Gil no longer turns negative. Gil is held at 1,000,000,000.
- NV+ forms added from the Japanese version can be used as fusion material.
