# Exvius Offline v0.1.91 (Beta)

## Dark Visions and The Spearmaiden

- Dark Visions battles now drop 10 to 100 Dark Matter on every clear, depending on the stage.
- Trade 1 Star Quartz for 1 Dark Matter in King Mog's Star Quartz Exchange, with no purchase limit.
- The Spearmaiden is available in Vortex Challenges. Clear three difficulties and battle Samatha for a chance to recruit her.

## Performance, Crashes & Navigation

- Fixed severe scroll lag when dragging on the **Summon** screen (especially under Limited Time), and fixed Step-Up lap reset buttons showing a stale summon result.
- Fixed a crash when renaming a save slot in **Save Management** and returning to the Title screen.
- Fixed **Dimensional Vortex** battles (including Chambers and Repeat runs) sending you back to the World Map instead of returning to the Vortex stage list.
- Fixed the **Season 2 Forbidden Land** cutscene loop and all other multi-part story cutscenes, plus fixed two broken Season 4 story cutscene triggers and the broken banner artwork on **Sea Hydra**.
- In **Rookie Panel Quest**, claim the current board’s tile, line, and full-board rewards to reveal the next board. You can revisit completed boards.
- Fixed **Lapis** reward displays on the Battle Result screen when a stage awards Lapis from both mission objectives and clear rewards, so the total shown matches the amount credited.

## Units, Abilities & Field Effects

- Fixed **NV+ Awakening** for **Dark Knight Duane**, **Blade of Vengeance Ibara**, **Eternal Radiance Elena**, **Richt**, and **Hyoh & Panthera Ultimus** so each awakens into their own proper form, and added automatic save recovery for units affected by the previous bug.
- Restored all missing **Area / Field Effects** (including **Burst Radiance [Ihana]**), fixing crashes when opening field-effect skills in Unit Enhance or battle.
- Fixed **Hero's** Trust Master and Super Trust Master rewards, fixed nine broken Trust Passives (including **Red Wings Cecil**), added missing English text and Limit Burst names for the seven **Monster Hunter Explore** units, and added missing chain family icons for **Paladin of Grandshelt** and **Fenrir Knight Riesz**.

## Summons, Shops & Rewards

- Fixed the summon buttons and shown prices on **Raid & Event Box Summons**. Finishing a box opens the next box and then the unlimited pool. Also moved **Assault! Nef-Garmat!** into the **Collab** tab, and cleaned up summon pools and featured lineups (including adding **Silent Knight Charlotte** to the Christmas Domino Featured Summon).
- Restored the **Mystery Crystal Exchange** in **Shop → Exchange Shop** so you can trade Mystery Crystals 1:1 for **Master's Cards** (to unlock the Vision Card slot on 7★ units) and Emperor Gigantuars.
- Added the one-time **Happy Thanksgiving 2026** gift to Mail containing 10 **Rain's Turkeys** (a new battle consumable that revives or restores one ally to full HP and fills their Limit Burst gauge) and 120 **Tier 3 Forticites** (20 each of Ruby, Amber, Sapphire, Emerald, Pearl, and Amethyst).
- Added **Riko** and **Lann & Reynn** as free promotional Inbox gifts.

## Events, Summons & Adventurer Rewards

- Added eight English **Step-Up Summons** for Edward, Scar, Olivier, Kimblee, Cater, Final Fantasy I, II, and III, Setzer and Rydia, and Paladin of Grandshelt. Step-Up confirmations now show the Lapis charged for the current step.
- **Land Swarming with Omega Spawn** now awards Event Points from clears. View the reward ladder in the Vortex and claim earned rewards in Mail. The August Adventurer objectives and Blade Breaker Esther’s rewards use the same Event Point total.
- Extended the personal reward ladders for ten raids to **10,000,000 points**, with additional raid coins at the restored milestones.
- Added **FINAL FANTASY X Special Quests II** and both **Fighting Back Memories Special Quests** to Home.
- Reopened fourteen event exchanges through **King Mog** and the **Mog Minister** in the Farplane and the **Curiosity Collector Moogle** in Elmont Castle. The Farplane choices include Mana Mystery and Night of the Pumpkin. Star Quartz and Orbonne are still available.
- Opened all eight main chapters of **The Lost Mirror Image** and **A Daydream of the Roaring Sea** in Vortex Story Events.
- Fixed a duplicate first-clear reward in **Glaive Castle Exploration** on Hard.
- Restored **Paladin of Grandshelt’s** Super Limit Burst forms and corrected the names of **Volition Explode**, **Ceodore’s Armor**, and **Ursula’s Chinese Dress**.
