## Exvius Offline v0.1.90 (Beta) — Data r34

### Story & Gameplay Fixes
- Fixed a softlock in **Season 2 (Forbidden Land)** where the cutscene after defeating Sol looped endlessly, even when tapping Skip or restarting the app. Also fixed the same multi-scene transition issue across all other multi-part story cutscenes. Already-stuck saves recover automatically on launch.
- Fixed **Daily Quests** not counting progress for Vortex stages, Explorations, Story quests, Summons, Gil earned, Crafting, and Colosseum wins. Claiming Daily Quest rewards now updates completion status and badges immediately.

### Summons, Notices & Companions
- Fixed **Paladin of Grandshelt** missing from his Featured Summon pool and Drop Rates table, and corrected overly high featured NV drop rates on eight Featured Summon banners back to the standard 1% per featured unit.
- Added a **5,000 Lapis** option to unlock Summon banners whose step-up lap limit or purchase limit has been reached. Tapping a locked banner opens a confirmation prompt and takes you straight into the unlocked banner.
- Updated the in-game **Notices** screen: renamed the first tab to **Change Log**, added a **F.A.Q.** guide under the **Important** tab, and cleaned up release notes into plain player-friendly text.
- Added a new permanent supporter companion: **Xander Stone** (26 companions total).
